Who He Is

John Constantine is the lead of DC Comics' Hellblazer: a working-class occultist from Liverpool who lives in London and survives on cunning, nerve and a pack of cigarettes. He is rarely the strongest person in a room, so he wins by lying well and thinking three moves ahead.

The look is what makes him easy to spot in a crowd: a rumpled trench coat, a loosened tie, a tired face and a cigarette that never seems to leave his hand.
